# Intelligent Gem Cutting Robotic Arm

> Research Project Website · SLIIT AIMS Research Group · RP-25J-231

A final year research project from the **Department of Information Technology**, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT), Malabe - developing an AI-powered robotic arm system for precision gem cutting using computer vision and deep learning.


---

## Project Overview

This project integrates robotics, computer vision, and machine learning to automate the gem cutting process with high precision. It is conducted under the **Autonomous Intelligent Machines and Systems (AIMS)** research group at SLIIT, with data collection support approved by the **Gem and Jewellery Research and Training Institute (GJRTI)**.

All data collected is strictly limited to research purposes and is non-commercial.
